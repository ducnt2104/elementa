# ELEMENTA Art Direction & Visual Style Guide

## 1. Core Visual Fantasy & Aesthetic Pillar
ELEMENTA takes place in the **Periodic Rift (Khe Nứt Tuần Hoàn)**—a high-tech, sacred elemental crucible where chemistry and quantum physics manifest as tangible combat forces. The visual tone is:
- **Scientific High Fantasy (Khoa học Kỳ ảo Vi mô):** Atomic structures, crystal lattices, electron orbitals, and chemical reactions meet top-down tactical MOBA arena readability.
- **Strict Readability Hierarchy:** Characters > Combat VFX > Structural Objectives (Towers/Cores) > Terrain & Foliage > Ambient Lighting.
- **Anti-Generic AI Principles:** Avoid muddy purple washes and unstructured neon blooms. Every color represents an exact chemical property (Redox, Electronegativity, Ionization, Thermodynamics).

## 2. Chemical Semantic Palette Tokens
- **Sodium (Na) / Alkali Metals:** Radiant Solar Flare `#f59e0b` (Amber Gold), `#fbbf24`, energetic sparks, rapid plasma discharge.
- **Iron (Fe) / Transition Metals:** Ferromagnetic Blue-Steel `#64748b`, `#38bdf8`, geometric magnetic field arcs, industrial forged plates.
- **Chlorine (Cl) / Halogens:** Corrosive Pale Yellow-Green `#84cc16`, `#a3e635`, acrid vapor plumes, high-contrast toxic trails.
- **Copper (Cu) / Conductors:** Antique Burnished Copper `#c2410c`, `#fb923c`, teal patina verdigris `#2dd4bf`, arc lightning.
- **Aluminum (Al) / Passivation:** Brilliant Silvery Pearl `#e2e8f0`, hexagonal corundum sapphire shields `#0284c7`.
- **Oxygen (O) / Oxidizers:** Combustion Crimson `#ef4444`, ozone violet `#a855f7`, oxygenating flame crowns.
- **Hydrogen (H) / Reducing Agents:** Luminescent Cyan `#00f0ff`, deep space plasma `#06b6d4`, high-speed cavitation trails.
- **Barium (Ba) / Heavy Alkaline Earth:** Dense Emerald Mineral `#10b981`, heavy crystalline BaSO₄ barite geomagnetism.

## 3. Team Identity & Health Semantics
- **Allied / Blue Team:** Primary `#00f0ff` (Cyan Ion), Secondary `#0284c7` (Cobalt Core), Health `#10b981` (Emerald Life).
- **Enemy / Red Team:** Primary `#ef4444` (Reaction Crimson), Secondary `#b91c1c` (Deep Hazard), Health `#f87171` (Hostile Ruby).
- **Neutral Objectives / Jungle:** Ancient Gold `#f59e0b`, Toxic Swamp `#84cc16`, Deep Amethyst `#8b5cf6`.
- **True Damage / Reactions:** Radiant White Gold `#ffffff` with `#fbbf24` core outline.

## 4. Silhouette & Visual Density Rules
1. **Characters (35-48px radius):** High-contrast circular base decal + distinct top-down silhouette (helmets, energy cores, weapon wings).
2. **Minions (14-22px radius):** Clear size hierarchy (Melee = Shield automaton; Ranged = Hovering spherical catalyst; Siege = Heavy tracked cannon walker).
3. **Turrets & Cores (40-80px radius):** Hexagonal monumental spires with spinning orbital gyroscopes and glowing energy cores.
4. **VFX Lifetime & Layering:** Background ground decals (Z=1) -> Units (Z=2) -> Projectiles (Z=3) -> Impact Bursts (Z=4) -> Floating Damage HUD (Z=5).
